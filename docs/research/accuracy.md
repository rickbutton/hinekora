# Hinekora against craftofexile.com

> Research notes (see `README.md`): the checker's reading of the game compared with
> craftofexile.com ("CoE"), the community's reference calculator, emulator and simulator,
> on 2026-11-08. Everything here is evidence; a conclusion that changes the checker moves to
> `ROADMAP.md`, `QUESTIONS.md` or a commit. PoE2 is out of scope; CoE was used in its
> `game=poe1` mode, data labelled "Allflame 3.29".

## What was compared, and how

- **CoE's data**, read in bulk rather than clicked through: `json/data/main/poec_data.json`
  (bases, modifiers, per-base tier tables with item levels, essences, fossils, 260 crafted
  modifiers), `poec_common.json` (bench costs), and the method table `poec_cmethods` plus the
  emulator's precondition engine `poec_updateActionEnabling` from `packages/package.js`.
  The emulator ("Wraecrafter Ultra") was then driven through its own functions
  (`poec_simSelectCurrency`, `poec_simApplyCraftGO`, `poec_simForceAction`) on a Vaal
  Regalia ilvl 84 to confirm effects, reading `crsim_data.iaffixes` and `crsim_dreasons`
  (the reasons a method is greyed out).
- **Hinekora**: `node packages/cli/dist/cli.js check` on 169 scratch crafts (three batches,
  `p01`–`p92`, `q01`–`q50`, `r01`–`r27`, kept outside the repo), and the pool through
  `@hinekora/core`'s `pool(catalog, emptyItem(base, ilvl, "rare"))`. Data snapshot:
  `repoe-fork/repoe @ 14e3edc89e` (2026-07-14; fossils and eldritch tables from
  2026-10-25). The manifest does not stamp the game version.
- **The wiki cache** arbitrates where the two disagree; the page is named in each row.

Verdicts: **AGREE**, **HINEKORA STRICTER** (rejects something the game allows: a false
positive), **HINEKORA LOOSER** (accepts something the game refuses, or claims a guarantee
the game does not give: a false negative, the serious kind), **DATA GAP**, **UNCLEAR**.

## 4. Essences

CoE's 25 essence types (21 tiered, Delirium, Horror, Hysteria, Insanity, Desolation) and
their per-class guaranteed modifiers were compared with `essences.json`'s `grants` at the
top tier (Deafening, or the single tier of the corrupted ones) for 13 item classes (body
armour, helmet, gloves, boots, shield, ring, amulet, belt, quiver, wand, bow, one-hand
sword, two-hand axe): **every cell agrees** (for example Greed: body/helmet/gloves/boots
"+# to maximum Life", ring/amulet "Regenerate # Life per second", belt "Flask Life
Recovery rate", weapons "Physical Attack Damage Leeched as Life"; Zeal: boots "Movement
Speed", body "Totem Placement speed", helmet "Warcry Speed"). The set of tiers per type
also agrees (Anger has no Whispering tier, Dread/Envy/Misery/Scorn start at Screaming,
and so on), and the class coverage per type agrees (CoE's "Rune Dagger" and "Warstaff"
labels fold into Hinekora's Dagger and Staff classes). Hinekora's `essence "<type>" t<n>`

## 5. Crafting bench

For body armour, CoE lists 58 crafted modifiers; Hinekora's `bench.json` resolves to the
same 58 families with the same sides, tier counts and level ladders (life 15/40/68/75/80;
single resistances 15/30/50; hybrid resistances 30/50/75; Jun's hybrids 60/72/81; the
five metamods at level 1). Group labels differ where CoE uses its own
(`DexterityIntelligence` for "+# to Dexterity and Intelligence", which Hinekora reads as
groups `Dexterity, Intelligence` from the game data: Hinekora is the stricter and, per the
wiki's group rule, the correct reading). Rules confirmed on the emulator: a craft whose
group is present is blocked (`blktrue`); a second craft without multimod replaces the
first; with multimod a fourth is refused; essence and fossil are prevented by every
metamod but multimod; scour under "Prefixes Cannot Be Changed" keeps the prefixes and the
item is Rare when two remain, Magic when one. Hinekora matches each. Hinekora accepts a
bench craft regardless of item level (`bench "maximum life"` on an ilvl 1 Rare checks);
CoE's tier tables give each crafted tier a level but the PoE1 emulator enforces none
(its `currencyModLevelFilter` is PoE2-only), so no verdict: UNCLEAR, low stakes.

## 6. Fossils

CoE's 21 weighted fossils and Hinekora's 25 (plus Bloodstained, Fractured, Gilded, Tangled)
agree on every "no X" block list up to the tag subsumption noted in §3; Hinekora also
carries Faceted's three added rows, Hollow's forced row, Glyphic's corrupted-essence
chance, Fractured's lock and Bloodstained's corruption. Weight multipliers ("more") are
not modelled, by design.
